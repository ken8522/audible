aax2text · cloud guide

Audiobook → text, in the cloud

Use Google Colab — a free Google website that lends you a fast computer — to turn your audiobook into text in minutes, without tying up your own PC.

What you need (all free):
• A Google account (the same one you use for Gmail) • The file audiobook_to_text_colab.ipynb (in this folder) • Your audiobook .m4b file
1

Open Google Colab and sign in

In your web browser, go to colab.research.google.com and sign in with your Google account.

2

Open the notebook

Click File → Upload notebook, then choose audiobook_to_text_colab.ipynb from this folder. A page full of labelled steps opens.

3

Turn on the free fast chip

Click Runtime → Change runtime type, pick T4 GPU, and click Save.

◆ Don't skip this — it's what makes it fast

The GPU is the fast chip that turns 5 hours of audio into a few minutes of work. Without it, the notebook still runs but crawls.

4

Run the steps one at a time

Click the round ▶ (play) button on the left of the first cell, wait for it to finish, then do the next one, top to bottom. They will, in order:

check the fast chip → install the transcriber → let you upload your .m4b (click “Choose Files”) → transcribe it (watch the % climb) → download your text file.

5

Get your text file

When the last step runs, a .txt file saves to your computer’s Downloads folder — split by chapter. Open it in Notepad or Word. Done! 🎉

Good to know

  • How long? On the free GPU, a 5-hour book takes roughly a few minutes.
  • Keep the tab open until it finishes. Closing it disconnects the cloud computer.
  • Big file won’t upload? Use the “Google Drive” cell in the notebook instead of “Choose Files” (also gentler on memory).
  • Long book / “out of RAM”? The notebook transcribes one chapter at a time and saves as it goes, so it stays light and can’t lose progress.
  • Want better wording? In the transcribe step, change MODEL = 'small' to 'medium'.
  • Privacy — this sends your audio to Google to do the work. Want nothing to leave your PC? Use the desktop version (see CHEAT_SHEET.html).
  • A red error? Re-read the note above that cell — usually it’s the GPU (Step 3) not turned on.
